The Founder

A vision shaped by purpose.

Aithan Jeshurun is the founder of Aithan Brands and Aithan Distributors. His brands include Aithan Automobiles, Aithan Engines & Machineries, Aithan Foods and Aithan Oils.

A Kingdom man known for charity and philanthropy, Aithan brings together entrepreneurship, faith, service and a strong sense of heritage. He is of Israeli-Hebrew descent and prefers a discreet personal presence.
