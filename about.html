Biography

Entrepreneurship with purpose.

Aithan Jeshurun is an entrepreneur and the founder of Aithan Brands and Aithan Distributors. Aithan Brands includes Aithan Automobiles, Aithan Engines & Machineries, Aithan Foods and Aithan Oils, bringing together ventures across key commercial sectors.

His entrepreneurial journey is complemented by a strong personal commitment to faith, responsibility, integrity and service. He is a Kingdom man whose values extend beyond business achievement.

Beyond business, Aithan Jeshurun is known for charity and philanthropy. His commitment to giving and helping others is an important part of the public story presented through this profile.

Heritage and identity

Aithan Jeshurun is of Israeli-Hebrew descent. His heritage is an important part of his identity and personal story.

A discreet public identity

Aithan Jeshurun prefers to maintain a private personal presence. Accordingly, this profile uses the official emblem as its visual identity and focuses on information relevant to his public business and philanthropic interests.
